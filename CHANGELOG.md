# Changelog

## 2.1.0 — 23 November 2026

- Replaced noisy keyword extraction in resume workflow with editable, role-specific requirement review.
- Added per-requirement proof confirmation, evidence edits, manual requirement entry and live scores.
- Rebuilt resume rewrite to preserve factual source content rather than manufacturing job-title/keyword claims.
- Added no-key LinkedIn draft variations and post-to-image shortcuts; retained optional Gemini integration.
- Added professional README/website banner and new usage guidance.

**Limitations:** These are explainable application-specific heuristics, not a certified ATS. PDF text extraction and AI output require human review. Publishing to LinkedIn is not automated without an approved integration.
